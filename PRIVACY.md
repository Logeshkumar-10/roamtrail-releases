# RoamTrail privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

## Who we are

RoamTrail is a personal travel tracker made by Logeshkumar Sivakumar (contact: elogu2001@outlook.com). There is no RoamTrail server. We do not receive, see or sell your data.

## What the app records, and where it stays

Everything below is stored on your phone, in the app's own database. Android Auto Backup is turned off for the app.

- **Your location while recording.** It is recorded only after you tap Start tracking, including in the background while the phone is locked, so your trips can be drawn. Each point holds a position, accuracy, speed, heading and time. Points less accurate than 50 m are discarded.
- **What is worked out from your points:** journeys, the cleared fog map, covered roads and railway lines, how often you used each road, and walking and explored-area totals.
- **What you enter:**
  - vehicles, including an optional registration number
  - an optional profile (name, date of birth, sex)
  - fuel fills (with the position at that moment, if you leave "Save the location" on)
  - notes, as text only
  - trips, home zones and never-record zones
  - your settings
- **Voice notes** are turned into text on the phone. No audio is saved.
- **Receipt photos** are read on the phone and deleted straight away. No image is saved.
- **A short error log** of the last 10 problems. It never contains a location, and it is never sent or backed up.

You can stop recording at any time. Settings > Backup > Delete my data removes everything from the phone, and, if you choose, from your drive.

## What leaves the phone

- **Your own backup (only if you sign in).** Your recorded data, vehicles, profile and settings go to your own OneDrive app folder (Microsoft account) or your own Google Drive hidden app-data folder (Google account). The app can see only its own folder. The files are protected by your account. Sign out, or revoke access in your account settings, to stop it.
- **Map tiles.** While the map is on screen, the app downloads map tiles from OpenFreeMap. The requests show which part of the map you are looking at, plus your IP address.
- **OpenStreetMap services:**
  - **Nominatim:** a place name you search for. With Travel mode on (it is off by default), your approximate position, about once every 30 minutes, to learn which town you are in.
  - **Overpass:** the area of a city you chose to track, to download its roads and railway lines.
  - Neither service receives your recorded trips.
- **Google ML Kit** reads receipt photos on the phone. It sends Google technical details for diagnostics: phone model and Android version, app package and version, per-install identifiers, scan timing, image size and format, and error codes, over HTTPS. Google's disclosure does not list the photo or the recognised text among them (developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure, last modified 14 May 2025, read 24 November 2026).

We add no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs of our own.

## Your choices

- **Location:** you can refuse location access. The app still opens, but it cannot record.
- **Backup:** backup is optional.
- **Travel mode:** off by default.
- **Export:** you can export your trips as a GPX or GeoJSON file.
- **Delete:** you can delete everything with Delete my data. Uninstalling the app also removes the data on the phone.

## Children

RoamTrail is not directed at children.
